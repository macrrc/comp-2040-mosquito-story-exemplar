# Assignment 2: The Provincial Bird

**The question:** which Winnipeg summers are worst for mosquitoes, and when and where is it worst?

**Findings:**

1. 2016 was the worst summer (28 per trap per count day), 2017 the quietest
   (3).
2. 9 of 11 summers peaked in June or July; the worst day was August 29,
   2022 (235 per trap).
3. The North West is the buggiest side of the city, by about three per trap: it's the whole city.
